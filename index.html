About

I am a fifth-year PhD student in Economics at Princeton University.

Prior to Princeton, I was a predoctoral research fellow at the Stanford Institute for Economic Policy Research (SIEPR), where I worked with Professor Heidi Williams. I received my BA in Economics, Mathematics, and Mathematical Methods in the Social Sciences (MMSS) from Northwestern University in 2020.

My primary research interests are in applied econometrics and labor.
